# VyaparMedia - CA & Legal Documents Guide (Simple Hinglish)

> **Platform:** VyaparMedia Technologies Private Limited  
> **Business Type:** Influencer Marketing & Escrow Marketplace (Brands + Creators)  
> **Model:** Razorpay Route / Escrow, Platform Commission Fee, Creator Payouts  

Yeh guide simple Hinglish me hai taaki aap aasaani se samajh sakein ki **CA (Chartered Accountant) ya CS (Company Secretary) aapke liye kya-kya documents banayega**, aur **aapko CA ko kya dena hoga**.

---

## 📌 Summary: Do Main Cheezein

1. **Aap CA ko kya doge:** Aapke personal documents (Aadhaar, PAN, Bank Statement, Photo) + Office ka address proof (Bijli bill, Rent agreement, NOC).
2. **CA aapko kya bana ke dega:** Company Certificate (COI), PAN/TAN, MOA/AOA, GST Number (Normal + TCS), Startup India Certificate, Net Worth Certificate, aur har mahine ke Tax/TDS returns.

---

## 1. Company Shuru Karte Waqt (Incorporation Stage)

Yeh saare documents CA / CS khud banata hai aur MCA (Govt portal) par file karta hai:

| Document ka Naam | Simple Bhasha me Matlab | Kaun Banayega? |
|---|---|---|
| **1. DSC (Digital Signature)** | Directers ke online digital sign (USB token ya certificate). Iske bina form sign nahi hota. | CA banwayega |
| **2. DIN (Director ID Number)** | Har director ka ek official ID number jo govt deti hai. | CA apply karega |
| **3. Name Approval** | "VYAPARMEDIA TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED" naam govt se pass karwana. | CA karega |
| **4. MOA (Memorandum of Association)** | Company ka constitution — isme likha hota hai ki company software, influencer marketplace aur tech platform ka business karegi. | CA / CS draft karega |
| **5. AOA (Articles of Association)** | Company ke internal rules (shares kaise batenge, directors ki powers kya hongi). | CA / CS draft karega |
| **6. SPICe+ Form** | Main government form jisme company apply hoti hai (is se PAN, TAN, EPFO sab ek sath nikalta hai). | CA bharega |
| **7. Certificate of Incorporation (COI)** | **Company ka Janam Praman Patra** — isme CIN (Company Identification Number) hota hai. Iske aate hi company officially ban gayi. | Govt/MCA (CA dega) |
| **8. Company PAN & TAN Card** | Company ka apna alag Income Tax PAN number aur TDS kaatne ke liye TAN number. | NSDL (CA nikal kar dega) |
| **9. Form INC-20A (Commencement of Business)** | **Bohot Zaroori:** Company banne ke baad jab aap bank me capital daal dete ho, to CA certify karta hai ki business officially start ho gaya. | **CA sign & certify karega** |
| **10. Share Certificates (Form SH-1)** | Founders/Shareholders ko official legal shares allot hone ka certificate. | CA / CS banayega |

## 2. Tax aur Sarkari Registration (Marketplace ke liye)

VyaparMedia normal shop nahi hai, yeh ek **E-Commerce / Creator Marketplace** hai jaha brands aur creators ke beech transactions hoti hain. Isliye yeh registrations CA se karwane honge:

| Registration | Kyun Chahiye? | Kaun Karega? |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Normal GST (REG-06)** | Jab aap brand se apni platform fee (commission) loge, to uspar 18% GST charge karne aur software/server (Vercel, AWS) ke GST ka ITC (credit) lene ke liye. | CA karega |
| **2. GST TCS Registration (Sec 52)** | **(Super Important):** E-commerce marketplace hone ke naate jab aap brand ka paisa leke creator ko dete ho, to aapko 0.5% - 1% TCS kaatna padta hai. Iske liye alag GST TCS ID lagti hai. | CA karega |
| **3. MSME / Udyam Registration** | Govt MSME certificate. Isse bank loan me aasani hoti hai, fees me rebate milti hai aur payment protection milti hai. | CA karega (1 din me) |
| **4. Startup India (DPIIT Certificate)** | Govt recognition certificate. Isse 3 saal tak income tax 0% (Sec 80-IAC) aur Angel Tax se chhoot milti hai. | CA file karega |
| **5. Professional Tax (PT)** | State Govt ka tax registration (company aur future employees ke liye). | CA karega |
| **6. Gumasta / Shop & Establishment** | Office address ke local municipal corporation ka basic business license. | CA / Local Agent |

## 3. Bank Account & Payment Gateway (Razorpay Route / Escrow)

Jab aap **Razorpay Route, Cashfree ya Bank Nodal/Escrow Account** ke liye apply karoge, to wo aapse yeh 4 documents maangenge:

1. **CA Net Worth Certificate:**
   * CA apne official letterhead par stamp aur **UDIN** ke sath likhega ki company aur directors ki net worth kitni hai. Yeh payment gateway mandatory maangta hai.
2. **Board Resolution for Bank Account & Escrow:**
   * Ek legal format jisme likha hota hai ki directors ne meeting karke bank current account aur Razorpay payment gateway tie-up approve kiya hai (CA bana ke dega).
3. **Capital Infusion Certificate:**
   * CA ka proof ki founders ne bank me initial share capital jama kar diya hai.
4. **Marketplace Flow & Escrow SOP Note:**
   * Ek 2-page document jo batata hai ki VyaparMedia customer ka paisa apne pass nahi rakhta, balki RBI rules ke mutabik Razorpay Route/Escrow ke zariye creator ko milestone complete hone par transfer karta hai.

## 4. Har Mahine aur Har 3 Mahine CA kya karega (Monthly & Quarterly)

Company banne ke baad regular tax compliances CA sambhalega:

### A. GST Returns:
* **GSTR-1 (Har mahine):** VyaparMedia ne kitni commission kamayi aur kitne bills kate, uski entry.
* **GSTR-3B (Har mahine):** Jo GST brand se collect kiya, usme se server/AWS ka GST minus karke baki govt ko jama karna.
* **GSTR-8 (Har mahine):** Creators ko jo payout gaya, uspar jo TCS kaata gaya uski monthly report.

### B. TDS Compliances (TDS Section 194-O & 194J):
* **TDS Deduction (Section 194-O):** Marketplace rule ke mutabik jab creator ko payout hota hai, to 1% TDS kaatna hota hai (agar PAN na ho to 5% under Sec 206AA).
* **Form 26Q (Quarterly):** Har 3 mahine me kate hue TDS ka govt portal par return bharna.
* **Form 16A:** Creators ko TDS certificate download karke dena taaki wo apna tax claim kar sakein.
* **Advance Tax:** Har 3 mahine company ke profit par advance tax calculate karke jama karna.

## 5. Saal ke End me kya hoga (Yearly Audit & ROC)

Financial year khatam hone par (31st March ke baad) CA aur CS yeh banate hain:

1. **Balance Sheet & P&L Account:** Pure saal ka nafa-nuksan aur assets ka formal hisab.
2. **Statutory Audit Report:** CA physically/digitally check karke audit report dega ki sab sahi hai.
3. **Tax Audit Report (Form 3CD):** Income Tax department ke liye audit report.
4. **Form AOC-4:** Balance sheet ko MCA (Govt portal) par upload karna.
5. **Form MGT-7:** Company ka Annual Return (shareholders aur directors ki details) ROC me file karna.
6. **Director KYC (DIR-3 KYC):** Har saal sabhi directors ka Aadhaar/OTP se verification.
7. **Company ITR-6:** Private limited company ka annual Income Tax Return.

## 6. Aapko (Founder ko) CA ko kya-kya dena padega?

CA aapse yeh documents maangega. Aap bas yeh arrange karke unhe de dijiye:

### A. Directers ke Personal Documents (Dono Directors ke):
* [ ] PAN Card copy (Self-attested sign karke)
* [ ] Aadhaar Card copy (Mobile number link hona chahiye OTP ke liye)
* [ ] Passport size photo
* [ ] Bank statement (pichle 2 mahine ka, jisme aapka naam aur ghar ka address saaf dikh raha ho)
* [ ] Mobile number aur Email ID

### B. Registered Office ke Documents:
* [ ] Office ka latest Bijli ka bill ya Paani ka bill (2 mahine se purana na ho)
* [ ] Office agar rented hai to: Rent Agreement
* [ ] Property owner ki taraf se **NOC** (No Objection Certificate - iska format CA khud de dega, bas owner se sign karwana hoga)

### C. Business Chalu Hone ke baad har mahine CA ko kya bhejoge:
* [ ] Company ke Bank Account ka statement (Excel + PDF)
* [ ] Razorpay / Payment Gateway ka monthly settlement report
* [ ] Server/Software ke bills (AWS, Vercel, Supabase, Google Workspace, etc.)
* [ ] Platform ke commission invoices
* [ ] Creators ko kitna paisa transfer kiya uski list (Creator Name, PAN, Amount, TDS)

## 7. Quick Step-by-Step Action Plan (Abhi kya karna hai)

```
Step 1: CA/CS choose karo
          │
Step 2: CA ko KYC do (Aadhaar, PAN, Bank Statement, Office Bijli Bill, NOC)
          │
Step 3: CA Name Approval aur SPICe+ form bharega (COI, PAN, TAN mil jayega)
          │
Step 4: Bank me Company Current Account kholo aur Share Capital deposit karo
          │
Step 5: CA INC-20A certify karega + GST & GST TCS apply karega
          │
Step 6: CA Net Worth Certificate le kar Razorpay Route / Payment Gateway live karo
```
