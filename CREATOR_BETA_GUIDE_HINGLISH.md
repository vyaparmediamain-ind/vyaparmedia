### 2.1 2-Minute Registration & Social Sync
1. `vyaparmedia.in/register` par jao aur **"Creator / Influencer"** select karo.
2. Apna Mobile Number OTP se verify karo.
3. Apna Instagram Creator/Business account aur YouTube channel connect karo. Isse tumhare real followers aur real engagement score automatic verify ho jayenge.

### 2.2 Paid Campaigns Dhundhna aur Proposal Bhejna
* Dashboard me **Campaigns** tab par jao. Wahan alag-alag brands (Clothing, Cafes, Fitness, Tech) ke paid offers dikhenge.
* Jo campaign pasand aaye uspe **"Apply"** dabao.
* Apna rate quote karo (jaise 1 Reel ke ₹3,500) aur pitch message likh do.
* Brand accept karegi aur deal turant generate ho jayegi!

### 2.3 Digital Smart Contract (Zero Jhanjhat)
Har deal shuru hone se pehle platform ek digital agreement banata hai:
* Kitni reels/videos hain aur duration kya hai.
* Draft submit karne ki deadline kya hai.
* Payment kitna milega.
* Dono taraf se phone par hi 1-click digital sign hota hai. Yeh Indian Contract Act ke tehat legally binding hai.

### 2.4 Physical Product Seeding (Shiprocket Courier)
Agar brand ne koi sample ya product bhejna hai:
* Brand tumhare ghar ke address par **Shiprocket** se courier bhejegi.
* Tumhare dashboard me live courier tracking (AWB) dikhegi.
* Jab parcel ghar deliver ho jaye, tab dashboard me **"Confirm Receipt"** dabao. Content banane ki deadline product milne ke baad shuru hoti hai!

### 2.5 Video Draft & The 2-Revision Rule
* Video shoot karke pehle dashboard me draft video upload karo.
* **48 Hours Ka Time**: Brand ke paas draft review karne ke liye sirf 48 ghante hote hain. Agar 48 ghante me brand ne koi reply nahi diya, toh video **auto-approve** ho jati hai!
* **Sirf 2 Revisions Allowed**: Brand maximum 2 chhota-mota correction maang sakti hai. 2 se zyada revision ke liye brand ko **₹500 per extra revision** alag se pay karna padega.

### 2.6 Live Posting with Mandatory `#vyaparmedia` & `#ad`
Draft approve hote hi reel Instagram/YouTube par live karo:
* **Rule**: Caption me brand ka tag (jaise `@brandname`), ASCI tag (**`#ad`** ya **`#collab`**), aur platform tracking tag **`#vyaparmedia`** dalna zaroori hai.
* Post live karke link dashboard me daalo aur **"Verify Post"** click karo.
* VyaparMedia ka automated API engine real-time me video check karke verify kar dega!

### 2.7 Instant Bank Payout (UPI / IMPS)
* Post verify hote hi Escrow se paisa tumhare **VyaparMedia Wallet** me aa jayega.
* **"Withdraw"** button dabao, apna UPI ID ya Bank Account Number daalo.
* **30 Seconds ke andar paisa tumhare bank account me hoga!**

# Page 3: Trust Score (DRS), Tax aur Lifetime Passive Kamai

### 3.1 DRS™ Trust Score: Jitna Achha Score, Utni Badi Deals
Platform par har creator ka ek **Trust Score (300 se 900 tak)** hota hai:
* **Score Kaise Badhta Hai**: Time par video dene se, bina lade deal complete karne se, aur genuine engagement se.
* **Score Kaise Girta Hai**: Deal accept karke gayab hone se ya fake bot followers lagane se.
* **Fayda**: Jin creators ka score 750+ hota hai, unhe search me sabse upar dikhaya jata hai, unhe bade D2C brands ke VIP campaigns milte hain, aur platform fee me discount milta hai.

### 3.2 TDS (Section 194J) & Pan Card: Paisa Sarkari Safe
* Government rules ke mutabiq creator payouts par 1% ya 2% TDS deduct hota hai.
* Agar tumne DigiLocker se apna PAN verify kar rakha hai, toh yeh kata hua TDS tumhare **Income Tax 26AS/AIS form** me jama hota hai. Saal ke end me ITR file karke yeh pura paisa government se wapas refund le sakte ho!
* Wallet se har deal ka GST/TDS statement 1-click me download ho jata hai.

### 3.3 Dispute Room: Agar Brand Pareshan Kare Toh?
Agar kabhi koi brand faltu me bol de ki "Mujhe video pasand nahi aayi" ya payment rokne ki koshish kare:
* Tum **"Raise Dispute"** button dabakar Arbitration Room me ja sakte ho.
* Platform ka neutral AI mediator aur human dispute officer contract terms check karega.
* Agar tumne contract ke hisaab se reel banayi hai, toh brand chahe ya na chahe, platform brand ke escrow se **tumhara pura payout release kar dega!**

### 3.4 5-Tier Referral System (Baithe-Baithe Lifetime Passive Kamai)
